    Ground rules on a reflux day

    • Warm, never iced. New York defaults to ice water and iced drinks. Say “no ice, room temperature” or “hot water, please”.
    • Zero caffeine. No coffee, black tea, green tea, matcha or energy drinks. Chamomile is fine; ginger tea in small amounts.
    • No grease, no spice, no acid. Skip fried food, chili oil, tomato sauce, citrus and vinegar. At Chinese places: “no chili oil, less oil, steamed please”.
    • Small portions, stay upright. Wait two to three hours after eating before lying down; raise the head of the bed if nights are bad.
    • This is not medical advice. Severe pain, vomiting blood, black stools or trouble swallowing: go to the ER or call 911.
